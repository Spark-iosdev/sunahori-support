About Sunahori

Dig the sand with your finger, pick up what you find, and spend your coins on tools. Each beach hides a single ring — find it to clear the beach. With gnomes, a bucket brigade, bomb drones and more, the digging keeps going even while you're away. Collect 5 fossils to bring a dinosaur back to dig alongside you, face each beach's twist (night, rain, crabs, gulls, tractors), and race other players in the world rankings (This Week's Time Attack, Daily Beach and Bare Hands). Available in Japanese, English, Traditional Chinese and Korean.

FAQ

How do I play?

Touch the sand with one finger to dig. Keep your finger down and it digs faster and wider. Use two fingers to move up and down and to zoom. You can see the guide anytime from "Beaches" → "How to play, support & privacy" at the bottom of the screen.

I bought "Remove Ads" but I still see ads

Tap "Restore Purchase" at the bottom of the Shop (or on the Beaches screen). If you're signed in with the same Apple Account, it's restored at no extra cost.

Can I move my progress to a new iPhone?

Game data is stored only on your device. It carries over when you restore your iPhone from a backup.

How do I join the rankings?

Open "Beaches" → "World Rankings". To post your times, sign in to Game Center in iPhone Settings → Game Center. You can still play the challenges without signing in. Your normal beach save isn't changed during a challenge, and you go back to it when you finish.

How do I get a dinosaur?

Dig deep on a beach (below about 30% of its depth) and that beach's dinosaur fossils start turning up. When you have all 5, restore it from "Collection" → "Fossils & Dinos" and it will dig with you.

Report an ad

To report an inappropriate or age-inappropriate ad, email leonbcgapartment@gmail.com with the subject "Sunahori ad report".

Please include when you saw it, where it appeared (banner, full-screen ad, away-reward video, etc.), what the ad showed, and the app version. You can attach a screenshot of the ad. Please don't include personal information.

You can also report ads from the in-app guide, just above the bottom banner, from the "Remove Ads" screen, and from the "While You Were Away" screen.
