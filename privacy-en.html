Privacy Policy (Sunahori)

1. Game data, storage and deletion

Your game progress (the state of the beaches you dug, coins, tools, Collection, etc.) is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer's servers. The developer does not automatically collect your name, email address or contact information through the app.

"Start over" on the Beaches screen resets the sand and find locations, coins, tools and Bag for that beach; your Collection and clear records are kept. To delete all game data, delete the app from iOS Settings using "Delete App" ("Offload App" keeps the data). Data included in backups can be managed and deleted in iOS / iCloud settings. The developer cannot delete data on your device remotely.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads at natural breaks, and video ads (rewarded ads) only when the player chooses to watch one. Ads are served by Google LLC's AdMob. Ads are shown with personalization turned off, and the app does not ask for tracking permission (ATT). Ad content is limited to a general-audience (G) rating.

To serve ads, prevent fraud, measure performance and diagnose problems, Google may process device and app information (such as IP address, device type, approximate region, ad impressions and interactions, app usage, and crash and performance information). Network communication may occur when the ad SDK starts up or loads ads, even if you don't watch a video.

How long Google keeps this information, how it is deleted, and how you can control your privacy are governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The away-reward video ad is optional; you receive the base reward without it. Purchasing or restoring "Remove Ads" stops the banner and full-screen ads and lets you get the 2× away reward without a video. Even with ads removed, the SDK may still communicate at startup. To stop all of the app's communication, including the SDK, stop using or delete the app.

You can report inappropriate or age-inappropriate ads from "Beaches → How to play, support & privacy → Report an inappropriate ad", just above the bottom banner, from the "Remove Ads" screen, and from the "While You Were Away" screen. If email isn't available, the "Report an ad" section of the support site opens instead.

3. In-app purchase

"Remove Ads" is a one-time, non-consumable purchase made through Apple's App Store. The developer never receives your payment information. Apple manages purchase records, and their retention and deletion follow Apple's policies and applicable law. Deleting the app or resetting a beach does not remove your Apple purchase history. Sign in with the same Apple Account and use "Restore Purchase" to restore it.

4. Withdrawing consent and stopping use

The app does not ask for notification or tracking permission and never makes such permissions a condition for rewards or features. You choose each time whether to watch a rewarded video, and you still receive the base reward if you don't. See "Advertising" above for how to stop automatically shown ads and how to stop the app's communication. Questions about data handling, withdrawing consent or deletion can also be sent to the contact below.

5. Protection of information by third parties

The third-party services used by this app (Google AdMob and the Apple App Store) provide, under their own privacy policies, a level of protection for personal data equal to or greater than this policy and the applicable App Store guidelines, including security, retention and deletion, and privacy controls for users. For details and how to exercise your rights, see the policies of Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

6. Children's privacy

The app has no chat or posting features. Minors should get a parent's or guardian's permission before making a purchase. You can also restrict purchases with Screen Time on iPhone.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page.

8. Contact and deletion requests

For questions about the app, inappropriate ads, or data handling, deletion or withdrawing consent, email leonbcgapartment@gmail.com. The reply address and content you send are used only to respond to you, kept only as long as needed, and then deleted except where retention is required by law. To request deletion of your inquiry, contact the same address. Information managed by Google or Apple may require you to contact them directly.
